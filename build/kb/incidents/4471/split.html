The fix queued for #4471 is the parser fix. It does not touch hint or one-day-move. Those reports will keep coming after it ships.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. This holds for the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it, then reads what people type the tenant’s way, day first. The people follow the hint and get the same flip as parser, through a different bug: the parser fix does not touch it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here.

one-day-move: dates move by one day, not by a day/month flip. Tuesday shows as Monday; an invite arrives before the booking; one meeting lands on two days for clients in Lisbon and Toronto; only June is off. No locale or form explains this, so it is not a parser problem. It looks like a time zone or daylight-saving shift. The desk had already marked these reports in desk-notes.json and footnoted them under parser; here they are a problem of their own.

How the split was made

  • Each report is counted once by its id. The export lists some twice.
  • The problem comes from the tenant, not the report: its locale (asked of the platform) and its booking form, as in causes.ts. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and the table shows how often it was wrong.
  • Every report with a date pair in its body was checked: all but the one-day moves are a day/month swap, and every weekday named matches its date.

Why one ticket hid three

Reports were merged by subject (decision 0001). Subjects like “Date is wrong” fit all three problems, so the merge could not tell them apart. The subject says how it felt; the tenant and the channel say what broke.
